The Project

Land that holds its
value — and its calm.

Saila Bhoomi is a hassle-free, fully converted residential venture laid out along the Hyderabad–Srisailam road, in the foothills of the Nallamala range. Every plot is part of an approved layout with clear title, so you buy an asset — not a question mark.

The site sits within a rare overlap: the spiritual pull of Srisailam, the forest and reservoir landscape of the Krishna basin, and the industrial growth corridor now forming around the proposed Regional Ring Road and Mucherla Pharma City. That combination is what makes this land both a retreat and an appreciating holding.

  • Approved by the Directorate of Town and Country Planning
  • Land conversion completed — certificates published and downloadable
  • Entrance, landscaping and beautification works actively under way
  • Backed by a listed developer with nearly four decades of corporate history

Why Saila Bhoomi

Six reasons this land is worth holding

Not a pitch — a checklist. These are the things that decide whether a plot is an asset or a liability.

01

Approved, not "approvable"

The layout is sanctioned by the Directorate of Town and Country Planning, and the land conversion orders are already issued. You can read both before you pay anything.

02

Clear & marketable title

Site selection is done by Country Condo's own team, and every venture is taken up only on land with clear title — the single biggest determinant of resale value.

03

On a growth corridor

Twenty minutes from the proposed Regional Ring Road and forty from the proposed Mucherla Pharma City — the two infrastructure moves reshaping land values south of Hyderabad.

04

A pilgrimage address

Srisailam — one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and eighteen Shakti Peethas — is the anchor of the region, drawing steady, year-round footfall.

05

Forest, hills and water

The Nallamala forest, the Srisailam reservoir, Akkamahadevi caves and Mallelatheertham falls are all within reach — scenery that does not get built over.

06

A developer with a record

Country Condo's Limited has been a corporate entity since 1987 and has delivered completed ventures — Coconut Grove, Country Kuteeram, Fairway, Sagar Sundervan among them.

Location & Connectivity

Where exactly is Saila Bhoomi?

The venture lies on the Hyderabad–Srisailam road, also reachable via the Hyderabad–Nagarjuna Sagar route — close enough to the city to be practical, far enough to be quiet.

  • 20 minutes Proposed Regional Ring Road (RRR)
  • 40 minutes Proposed Pharma City at Mucherla
  • On the highway Hyderabad–Srisailam road frontage access
  • Alternate approach Hyderabad–Nagarjuna Sagar road
  • The anchor Srisailam temple, dam and Sikharam
