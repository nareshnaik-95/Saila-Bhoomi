Our Story

From a 1987 incorporation to layouts in six states.

The company was originally incorporated as a private limited company in Andhra Pradesh in September 1987, and converted into a public limited company the following year. It later diversified into the real estate business as the sector expanded in India and abroad — and particularly in Hyderabad.

What began as a single-market operation now spans Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra and Rajasthan — plotted residential ventures placed near temples, highways, hill ranges and coastlines. The pattern is deliberate: land next to something permanent tends to hold its value better than land next to something speculative.

Our site selection team looks for locations where the title is clean, the conversion is achievable, and the surroundings give an owner a reason to actually visit. Then we put the approvals on the public record so buyers can check them without asking us for permission.

How We Work

Four things we do before a plot goes on sale

A layout is only as good as the work done before anyone is asked for money.

01 · Site selection Our own team surveys the land, the approach roads and the surrounding development before the company commits.
02 · Title & conversion Title is verified and the land is converted for residential use, with the conversion orders obtained in the company's name.
03 · Layout approval The plotting layout is sanctioned by the planning authority — and published as a downloadable PDF.
04 · Development works Entrance, internal roads, lighting and landscaping are taken up, with progress recorded and shared.

What We Hold To

Three principles, not a mission statement

01

Publish the paperwork

Conversion orders and approved layouts go online for every venture. A buyer who can verify does not need to be convinced.

02

Buy location, not hype

Temples, highways, reservoirs, hill ranges, university towns. We look for anchors that were there before us and will outlast us.

03

One point of contact

From first enquiry to registration, the same person handles your file. No passing you between desks.
